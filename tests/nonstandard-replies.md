# Three adversarial but reasonable learner replies

1. “I can add the request-only booking form at the current price if we remove the separate About page and six-image gallery. The total remains $1,500, due day 7. Would you accept that exact swap?” Expected: pending exchange; resolve IDs, verify both still present, 4 hours added and 4 removed. Explicit acceptance yields 20 hours, $75/hour estimated revenue. Do not remove items merely because the learner proposed it. Counterfactual: “remove two other things” requires clarification and no mutation.

2. “Which exact fields do you need in the booking form? Does a submission reserve a place, or will you confirm availability manually?” Expected: client reveals name/email/class/preferred date and manual confirmation. No fee, hours or deadline changes. No feedback penalty for not quoting immediately. Counterfactual: request for payment or live calendar requires a new supported scope definition, not silent inclusion in the 4-hour estimate.

3. “This change is already included in our contract. Why are we discussing an extra fee?” Expected: client distinguishes the included name/email/message contact form from extra class/date request fields. If the client only wants the original fields, acknowledge inclusion and retain the contract. If booking fields are wanted, explain the difference and invite options without forcing an upsell. No automatic penalty for challenging scope; evaluate the learner's decision after clarification.
